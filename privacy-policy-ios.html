Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: November 7, 2026 — describes the upcoming version 2.1.4 Unity LevelPlay release. Earlier released versions use Google AdMob, as noted below.

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play, by whom, and why. The short version: the game has no player accounts, and complete saves and photos stay on your device. Gameplay analytics and online content updates are disabled in this release. The app includes optional rewarded ads mediated by Unity LevelPlay, which play only when you choose to watch one, and optional in-app purchases processed by Apple.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins, trails, themes and tracks, relics, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded as a complete save. Gameplay statistics stay on your device. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never sent anywhere. The app also keeps a small local log of unexpected errors (error message, file name and line number, app version, platform) to help it recover gracefully. The full log stays on the device and is not sent to a gameplay analytics service.

Gameplay analytics and online content updates

This release does not load GameAnalytics, send gameplay analytics events, fetch remote gameplay settings, or download game-content updates. There is no gameplay analytics consent prompt or setting in this release. Game updates are delivered through the app store.

Disabling these features does not delete records already received during earlier testing. For questions about those records, contact martinkhch@gmail.com. Advertising and purchase services described below have their own data handling.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The upcoming release uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and ironSource for optional rewarded ads. An ad plays only when you choose an offered reward: a revive, doubled gems, daily gems, streak repair or a custom skin. There are no banners or automatic interstitial ads. You can play without watching ads.

Opening the game does not start the advertising SDKs or display an advertising consent screen. When you choose a rewarded ad, the app first checks regional privacy requirements using InMobi CMP. Where consent is required, its choices appear before ads can load. If required permission is missing, declined, or cannot be checked, ads remain unavailable and you can keep playing. Other regions do not receive a blanket consent prompt. Revisit Settings → Ad privacy options to review regional choices or disable optional ads. Existing local opt-outs are preserved. Changing privacy choices after an advertising SDK has started prevents further ad requests for that session; restart the app to use the new choice.

The regional privacy check contacts InMobi to download the app's consent configuration and determine the applicable region from the network connection. Consent records are stored on the device and may be refreshed when required by the CMP configuration. See InMobi's privacy policy for its data handling and privacy-rights information.

When you allow ads, Unity's advertising services (including LevelPlay's Ad Quality component) may process device, app and user identifiers, IP address and approximate region, device model, operating system, language, ad views, interactions, app usage times, purchase history and diagnostics. These support contextual ad delivery, measurement, service operation, ad quality and fraud prevention. This is separate from the disabled gameplay analytics feature. The game does not request precise location or send your custom photo or complete saved progress to advertising providers.

The app sends default requests for non-personalized ads and an opt-out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising. The regional consent form also sends your selected privacy choices directly to the configured ad providers using IAB TCF, Google's Additional Consent and US privacy signals. Those choices can take priority over the app's default requests; the defaults are not a guarantee that accepting every purpose excludes personalization. You can review your choices or turn rewarded ads off in Settings. Contextual ads also require data processing. You can review app tracking permissions in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

When you request your first rewarded ad, after any required regional privacy choices, iOS may separately ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before the advertising SDKs start. This request concerns using the device's advertising identifier for ad measurement across other companies' apps; non-personalized advertising can still involve this kind of measurement. You can decline Apple's request and still watch available rewarded ads and earn the same rewards. The app keeps its negative personalization consent and sale/sharing opt-out signals for either answer. It respects Apple's permission and does not substitute another identifier to bypass a denial. Apple's SKAdNetwork may also be used for attribution. Changing system tracking permission invalidates cached ads until restart.

If you choose to report an ad using a provider's reporting or support feature, the provider may also receive the report and information you submit for that request.

Read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for provider data handling, retention and privacy-rights requests. Earlier released versions use Google AdMob and remain subject to Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases (Apple)

The app offers optional in-app purchases — gem packs and the Ultra Pass — that are never required to play. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your name, payment card, billing address or any other payment details; Apple handles the payment and gives the app only a purchase confirmation. The app keeps a small local record of completed transaction identifiers on your device so that a purchase is not granted again when the store redelivers the same transaction. Restore Purchases in the shop restores eligible permanent purchases, such as Ultra Pass. Consumed gem packs are not restored as new gem grants. Apple's handling of your purchase data is described in App Store & Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

Version 2.1.4 may request Apple's tracking permission when you request your first rewarded ad; denying tracking does not remove ad or reward eligibility. It may also ask for notification permission to send an optional local reminder about your daily streak; the reminder is scheduled on your device and no server is involved. If you choose the "My Photo" skin, the photo you select is used only for that skin and stays on your device. Rating prompts, if shown, use Apple's standard in-app review dialog.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app requests non-personalized ads. If iOS restricts tracking permission, the app respects that restriction and keeps rewarded ads available.

Data deletion

This release has no player accounts or first-party save server. You can clear your locally saved progress from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset all progress (which also deletes your custom photo and the local error log), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Eligible permanent purchases stay associated with your Apple Account and can be restored after a reinstall; consumed gem packs cannot. Resetting local progress does not delete store or advertising-provider records. For provider data, use the privacy rights and contact options in their policies linked above, or contact us for help directing a request. For records from earlier gameplay-analytics testing, use the contact listed in that section above.
