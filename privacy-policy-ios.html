Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: June 22, 2026

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. The short version: the iOS version has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no analytics — it collects no data and transmits nothing off your device.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins and tracks, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere. The app also keeps a small local diagnostic log of unexpected errors on your device to help it recover gracefully; this log stays on the device and is never transmitted.

No advertising, no tracking

The iOS version of Loop Dodge shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you or request permission to track. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is accessed or used.

No purchases

The iOS version has no in-app purchases. All gems and unlockable content are earned by playing.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

The app keeps no accounts and no server-side data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can clear everything from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset progress (which also deletes your custom photo), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress.
