Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy (iOS)

Last updated: September 3, 2026 — applies to Loop Dodge for iOS version 2.1.0 and later

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play, by whom, and why. The short version: the game itself has no accounts, no servers and no analytics, and your progress never leaves your device. The only third-party data collection comes from two things you control: optional rewarded ads served by Google AdMob, which play only when you choose to watch one, and optional in-app purchases processed by Apple.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins, trails, themes and tracks, relics, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never sent anywhere. The app also keeps a small local log of unexpected errors (error message, file name and line number, app version, platform) to help it recover gracefully; this log contains no personal data, stays on the device and is never transmitted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. Ads in Loop Dodge are opt-in rewarded ads only: an ad plays only when you tap a button that offers an in-game bonus in exchange for watching it (for example a revive, doubled gems, free daily gems or a streak repair). There are no banner or interstitial ads, no ad ever appears unprompted, and you can play the whole game without ever watching one.

To serve, measure and secure ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only where iOS permits it. Loop Dodge never asks for permission to track you (it does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), so on the iOS versions the app supports the IDFA is not available to the SDK, and non-personalized ad signals or app-bounded identifiers are used instead.
  • Your IP address, from which Google derives your approximate (coarse) location, such as your country or region.
  • Ad interaction data (ads requested, shown, viewed and tapped) and basic device and app information (device model, OS version, language, app version).
  • Diagnostics (non-personal crash logs and performance data).

Because tracking permission is never requested, ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. Google may still select ads using non-identifying, contextual signals (such as your approximate region, device type and the app you are using), and may use Apple's privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork framework to measure ad campaigns without identifying you. Data sent to Google is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS).

How Google uses this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can manage Google's ad settings at Google Ads Settings and My Ad Center, and the list of AdMob's advertising partners is available here.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad is requested, and you can refuse personalized advertising; no ad is loaded unless your choice allows it. Where this applies, you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings → Ad privacy options. On your device you can also turn off personalized ads under Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising and keep tracking off under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

In-app purchases (Apple)

The app offers optional in-app purchases — gem packs and the Ultra Pass — that are never required to play. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store using your Apple Account. We never see or store your name, payment card, billing address or any other payment details; Apple handles the payment and gives the app only a purchase confirmation. The app keeps a small local record of completed transaction identifiers on your device so that a purchase is granted exactly once and can be restored (Restore Purchases in the shop). Apple's handling of your purchase data is described in App Store & Privacy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Device permissions

The app never requests permission to track you. It may ask for notification permission to send an optional local reminder about your daily streak; the reminder is scheduled on your device and no server is involved. If you choose the "My Photo" skin, the photo you select is used only for that skin and stays on your device. Rating prompts, if shown, use Apple's standard in-app review dialog.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests are always made without tracking permission and without the advertising identifier, as described above.

Data deletion

The app has no accounts and keeps no server-side data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can clear everything from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset all progress (which also deletes your custom photo and the local error log), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. Purchases stay associated with your Apple Account and can be restored after a reinstall. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at My Ad Center.
