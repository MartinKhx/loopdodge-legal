Loop Dodge — Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

Loop Dodge is a free arcade game where you guide a ball around looping tracks and dodge the hazards. If something isn't working or you have a question, get in touch and we'll get back to you.

Contact

Email us anytime at mxtinix@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem — it helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

Is it free? Yes. Loop Dodge is free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases. Everything is earned by playing.

How do I reset my progress? Open the game's Settings and choose Reset progress. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Where does my data go? Nowhere — all your progress is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
