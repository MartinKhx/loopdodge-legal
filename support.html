Loop Dodge — Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

Loop Dodge is a free arcade game where you guide a ball around looping tracks and dodge the hazards. If something isn't working or you have a question, get in touch and we'll get back to you.

Contact

Email us anytime at mxtinix@gmail.com and we'll reply as soon as we can. Please include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem — it helps us fix it faster.

Common questions

Is it free? Yes. Loop Dodge is free to play. There are no forced ads: the only ads are optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus, and you can play the whole game without ever watching one. Optional in-app purchases (gem packs and the Ultra Pass) are available but never required — everything can be earned by playing.

How do I restore a purchase? Open the in-game shop and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I reset my progress? Open the game's Settings and choose Reset all progress. Uninstalling the app also removes all locally stored data.

Where does my data go? Your progress is stored only on your device. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad, it is served by Google AdMob, which may collect limited device and ad data; purchases are handled by Apple. See our Privacy Policy for details.
