Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026 — describes the upcoming Android version 2.1.4 Unity LevelPlay release. Earlier released versions use Google AdMob as noted below.

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play, by whom, and why. The short version: the game has no player accounts. Saves and custom photos remain on your device. Gameplay analytics and downloaded game-content updates are disabled in this release. Advertising and purchases use the services described below.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins and tracks, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally. The complete save is never uploaded. Gameplay statistics stay on your device. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere. The app also keeps a small local log of unexpected errors (error message, file name and line number, app version, platform) to help it recover gracefully. The full log stays on the device and is not sent to a gameplay analytics service.

Automatic Android backup is disabled for this app, so none of this on-device data (including your photo) is copied to Google Drive or transferred to another device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of it.

Gameplay analytics and online content updates

This release does not load GameAnalytics, send gameplay analytics events, fetch remote gameplay settings, or download game-content updates. There is no gameplay analytics consent prompt or setting in this release. Game updates are delivered through the app store.

Disabling these features does not delete records already received during earlier testing. For questions about those records, contact martinkhch@gmail.com. Advertising and purchase services described below have their own data handling.

Advertising (Unity LevelPlay)

The upcoming release uses Unity LevelPlay with Unity Ads and ironSource for optional rewarded ads. An ad plays only when you choose an offered reward: a revive, doubled gems, daily gems, streak repair or a custom skin. There are no banners or automatic interstitial ads. You can play without watching ads.

Before starting the advertising SDKs, the app asks whether you want to allow optional ads. If you decline or do not make a choice, these SDKs do not start. Your choice and its date are stored locally and expire after 180 days. Revisit Settings → Ad privacy options at any time. Changing the choice after the SDK has started disables further ad requests for that session; restart the app to use the new choice.

When you allow ads, Unity's advertising services (including LevelPlay's Ad Quality component) may collect and share device, app and user identifiers, IP address and approximate region, device model, operating system, language, ad views, interactions and diagnostics. Unity's SDK disclosures also include purchase history for advertising and measurement. These services use this information for ad delivery, measurement, service operation, ad quality and fraud prevention. This is separate from the disabled gameplay analytics feature. The game does not create an advertising user account, explicitly forward purchase events to the ad SDKs, request precise location, or send your custom photo or complete saved progress to advertising providers.

The app requests non-personalized ads, sends a negative personalization consent signal, and opts out of sale/sharing for targeted advertising. Contextual ads still require data processing. This is an in-app choice for optional contextual advertising, not an IAB TCF consent platform. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device privacy settings.

Read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for provider data handling, retention and privacy-rights requests. Earlier released versions use Google AdMob, which processes identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics under Google's privacy policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The app offers optional in-app purchases — gem packs and Ultra Pass — that are never required to play. Earlier versions also supported Remove Ads; existing permanent entitlements are retained. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play using your Google account. We never see or store your name, payment card, billing address or any other payment details; Google Play handles the payment and gives the app only a purchase confirmation. The app keeps a small local record of completed transaction identifiers on your device so that a purchase is not granted again when the store redelivers the same transaction. Restore Purchases in the shop checks eligible permanent purchases, such as Ultra Pass. Consumed gem packs are not restored as new gem grants. Google's handling of your purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

This release has no player accounts or first-party save server. You can clear your locally saved progress from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset all progress (which also deletes your custom photo), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. Resetting local progress does not delete store or advertising-provider records.

To request help deleting data associated with Loop Dodge, email mxtinix@gmail.com with the subject "Loop Dodge data deletion" and say which service your request concerns. We can help direct the request to the provider that holds the records. Do not send passwords or payment details.

For Unity advertising records, use the privacy controls available from the ad's Data Privacy icon, or the request instructions in Unity's policy. For records held by Google, use the privacy controls and contact options in Google's policy. Providers may verify your request and retain records required for transactions, legal obligations or fraud prevention under their policies; clearing local progress does not erase those records. For earlier gameplay-analytics testing, use the contact listed in that section above.
