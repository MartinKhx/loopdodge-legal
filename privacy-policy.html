Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 — applies to the upcoming Appodeal release with gameplay analytics and online content updates disabled.

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play, by whom, and why. The short version: the game has no player accounts. Saves and custom photos remain on your device. Optional gameplay analytics, advertising, purchases, and update downloads use the services described below.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins and tracks, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally. The complete save is never uploaded. Gameplay statistics stay on your device. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere. The app also keeps a small local log of unexpected errors (error message, file name and line number, app version, platform) to help it recover gracefully. The full log stays on the device and is not sent to a gameplay analytics service.

Automatic Android backup is disabled for this app, so none of this on-device data (including your photo) is copied to Google Drive or transferred to another device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of it.

Gameplay analytics and online content updates

This release does not load GameAnalytics, send gameplay analytics events, fetch remote gameplay settings, or download game-content updates. There is no gameplay analytics consent prompt or setting in this release. Game updates are delivered through the app store.

Disabling these features does not delete records already received during earlier testing. For questions about those records, contact martinkhch@gmail.com. Advertising and purchase services described below have their own data handling.

Advertising (Appodeal)

The upcoming release uses Appodeal to mediate optional rewarded ads, with BidMachine, AppLovin and Unity Ads included as advertising partners. An ad plays only when you choose an offered reward, such as a revive, doubled gems, daily gems, streak repair or a custom skin. There are no banner or automatic interstitial ads. You can play without watching ads.

Appodeal and the participating advertising providers may process device and app identifiers, your IP address and approximate region, device model, operating system, language, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic information. They use this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and operate their advertising services. This advertising reporting is separate from the disabled gameplay analytics feature. The game does not request access to precise device location or send your photo or complete saved progress to advertising providers.

The app requests non-personalized ads. This does not eliminate data processing needed for contextual ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device's privacy settings. Apple's SKAdNetwork may be used for privacy-preserving attribution on iOS.

Where consent or privacy choices are required, the app uses Appodeal's Stack Consent Manager and Google User Messaging Platform before requesting ads. If that process cannot complete, ads remain unavailable and gameplay continues. Where offered, revisit Settings → Ad privacy options to change your choices. Ads are paused for the rest of that app session after opening these options, so cached ads cannot be reused with older privacy choices. Restart the app to request new ads under the updated choices.

Read the providers' policies for their data handling, retention and privacy rights: Appodeal, BidMachine, AppLovin, Unity, and Google (consent management). Earlier released versions use Google AdMob; those versions remain subject to Google's advertising privacy policy.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The app offers optional in-app purchases — gem packs, the Ultra Pass and Remove Ads — that are never required to play. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play using your Google account. We never see or store your name, payment card, billing address or any other payment details; Google Play handles the payment and gives the app only a purchase confirmation. The app keeps a small local record of completed transaction identifiers on your device so that a purchase is granted exactly once and can be restored (Restore Purchases in the shop). Google's handling of your purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

The app has no accounts and keeps no server-side data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can clear everything from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset all progress (which also deletes your custom photo), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. For data processed by advertising providers, use the privacy rights and contact options in their policies linked above.
