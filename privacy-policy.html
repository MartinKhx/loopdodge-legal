Loop Dodge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Loop Dodge ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data is collected when you play, by whom, and why. The short version: the game itself has no accounts, no servers, and uploads nothing — the only third-party data collection comes from the advertising SDK and the Google Play purchase system described below.

Data stored on your device only

Your progress (best score, gems, unlocked skins and tracks, missions, daily streak, settings) is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. If you unlock the "My Photo" ball skin, the photo you pick is processed and stored only on your device — it is never uploaded anywhere. The app also keeps a small local log of unexpected errors (error message, file name and line number, app version, platform) to help it recover gracefully; this log contains no personal data, stays on the device and is never transmitted.

Automatic Android backup is disabled for this app, so none of this on-device data (including your photo) is copied to Google Drive or transferred to another device. Uninstalling the app permanently removes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service by Google. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers (including the advertising ID)
  • Approximate (coarse) location, derived from your IP address
  • App interaction data (e.g. ad views and clicks)
  • Diagnostics (crash logs, performance data)

This data is used for advertising and marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS). How Google uses this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and the list of AdMob's advertising partners is available here.

In the European Economic Area and the UK, a consent form (Google UMP) is shown before any ad is loaded, and you can refuse personalized advertising. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Google settings.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

The app offers optional in-app purchases — gem packs, the Ultra Pass and Remove Ads — that are never required to play. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play using your Google account. We never see or store your name, payment card, billing address or any other payment details; Google Play handles the payment and gives the app only a purchase confirmation. The app keeps a small local record of completed transaction identifiers on your device so that a purchase is granted exactly once and can be restored (Restore Purchases in the shop). Google's handling of your purchase data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Data deletion

The app has no accounts and keeps no server-side data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can clear everything from inside the app at any time using Settings → Reset all progress (which also deletes your custom photo), and uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress. For data collected by Google AdMob, see Google's privacy controls at My Ad Center.
